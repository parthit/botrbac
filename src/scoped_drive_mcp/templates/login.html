Sign in to Scoped Drive

Sign in with your own Google account. You pick your own files, and the assistant uses this same account. Each person’s files stay separate.

Scoped Drive will ask Google for your email address, so this server knows which list is yours, and for access to your Drive. An assistant can open only the files and folders you allow. Those contents go only to an assistant you connect. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train a model.

This is described in the privacy policy and the terms of service.
