Sign in.

Sign in with your own Google account. You pick your own files, and the assistant uses this same account. Each person’s files stay separate.

Scoped Drive will ask Google for your email address, so this server knows which list is yours, and for access to the files and folders you pick in Google Drive. File contents of those items go only to an assistant you connect. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train a model.

This is described in the privacy policy and the terms of service.
