Scoped Drive is a gateway that sits in front of Google Drive. You sign in with your own Google account, pick the files and folders an assistant may use, and this server refuses the rest.

Signing in asks Google for your email and for access to the files and folders you pick. Contents of those items go only to an assistant you connect. They are not sold, used for advertising, or used to train a model.

The boundary is the product.

Three checks. No extra Google products.

  • Only what you pick. Google opens the files you choose in the picker, plus files an assistant creates here. This server checks the list again.
  • Encrypted tokens. The Google refresh token is encrypted before it is stored. File contents are not kept on this server.
  • Revoke anytime. Untick an item, disconnect an app, or remove Scoped Drive from your Google Account. New calls stop. Anything already sent is not recalled.
