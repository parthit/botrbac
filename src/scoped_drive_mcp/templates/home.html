Exactly what Bot RBAC can see

Signing in asks Google only for your email. Connecting Google Drive is a separate step: Google then lets Bot RBAC open only the files and folders you pick in Google’s file picker, plus files it creates for you. The rest of your Drive stays closed to it. Of the files you pick, your assistant can open only the ones on your Allowed list. Notion and OneDrive are optional, each with its own permission screen and its own list. File contents go only to the assistants you connect. They are not stored here, sold, used for advertising, or used to train a model.

The boundary is the product.

Three checks. No extra Google products.

  • Only what you allow. Bot RBAC can only open the files you pick in Google’s file picker. Your assistant can only open the ones on your Allowed list. Every request is checked against that list.
  • Encrypted tokens. The Google refresh token is encrypted before it is stored. File contents are not kept on this server.
  • Revoke anytime. Untick an item, disconnect an app, disconnect a service, or remove Bot RBAC from your Google Account. New calls stop. Anything already sent is not recalled.
