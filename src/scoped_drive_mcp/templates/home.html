Give an assistant only the Drive files you choose

Scoped Drive is a gateway that sits in front of Google Drive. You sign in with your own Google account, pick the files and folders an assistant may use, and this server refuses the rest.

Signing in asks Google for your email and for access to the files and folders you pick. Contents of those items go only to an assistant you connect. They are not sold, used for advertising, or used to train a model.

How it works

  1. 1 Sign in with your own Google account. Each person’s files stay separate.
  2. 2 Pick the files and folders an assistant may use. Remove one whenever you want.
  3. 3 Connect Grok, Muse, or Cursor, then approve the connection when this site asks.

Why you can trust the boundary

Only what you pick

Google grants this app the files you choose in the picker, plus files an assistant creates here. This server still checks every call against your list.

Encrypted tokens

The Google refresh token is encrypted before it is stored. File contents are not kept on this server.

Revoke anytime

Untick an item, disconnect an app, or remove Scoped Drive from your Google Account. New calls stop. Anything already sent is not recalled.
