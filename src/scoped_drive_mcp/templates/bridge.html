Mailbox

This pairs your assistant with one other person’s assistant. Both of you have to approve. Messages are short text. They stay with your assistant, which should summarize them for you and wait. The other person’s Google account is the name you are confirming.

While a pairing is on, that person’s assistant can also read the items on your Sharing list (read-only), and yours can read theirs. Nothing else in your Drive is reachable.
