Or, in Safari, turn off Prevent cross-site tracking in Safari → Settings → Privacy while you pick, then turn it back on: files you’ve picked stay picked.

On iPhone and iPad, Google’s file picker opens on Google’s own page and brings you back here when you’re done. (it needs cross-site tracking allowed, see below).

This device uses the in-page picker, which needs cross-site tracking allowed (see below).

For the in-page picker on iPhone or iPad: in the Settings app, go to Apps → Safari and turn off Prevent Cross-Site Tracking while you pick, then turn it back on: files you’ve picked stay picked. In another browser, turn on Allow Cross-Website Tracking for it instead.
