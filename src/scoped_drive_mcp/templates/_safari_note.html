{#- Safari blocks Google's cookies inside the picker's frame while "Prevent cross-site tracking" is on, and Google then shows "Can't access your Google Account". This page can't see inside that frame, so ui.js shows the note to every Safari visitor, with the Mac or the iPhone/iPad steps (every iOS browser runs on Safari's engine). -#}

Using Safari? Google’s file picker can’t load while Safari’s Prevent cross-site tracking is on. Turn it off in Safari → Settings → Privacy while you pick, then turn it back on: files you’ve picked stay picked. Or use another browser. On iPhone or iPad, Google’s file picker can’t load while cross-site tracking is blocked. In the Settings app, go to Apps → Safari and turn off Prevent Cross-Site Tracking while you pick, then turn it back on: files you’ve picked stay picked. In another browser, turn on Allow Cross-Website Tracking for it instead.
